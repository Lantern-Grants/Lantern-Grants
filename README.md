## Lantern Grants

A free, public search of open research funding calls, and of who funds work like yours. Every listing links to the funder's own page, and you don't need an account to use it.

- **Open funding calls:** NIH, NSF, the EU, UKRI, foundations and more, checked twice a day. https://lanterngrants.com/funding
- **Who funds work like yours:** paste an abstract and see funded projects like it and who paid for them. https://lanterngrants.com/who-funds-this
- **For nonprofits:** which private foundations say they accept applications, state by state, from public IRS filings. https://lanterngrants.com/foundations

Our sister site, [Lantern Scholarships](https://lanternscholarships.com), is a free scholarship list for students.
